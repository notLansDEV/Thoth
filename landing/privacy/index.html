Privacy Policy

Thoth is a self-hosted application. Your data stays on your own server and infrastructure.

Data ownership

When you self-host Thoth, all project data, accounts, tasks, bugs, files, and content are stored in your own database and storage volumes. The Thoth project does not collect or host your instance data.

Data stored by your instance

  • Account information you create (username, email, password hash, profile data).
  • Workspace/project content you add (projects, tasks, bugs, milestones, reports, Markdown, attachments).
  • Operational data needed for authentication, permissions, and audit/security logs.

Cookies and local storage

Thoth may use browser cookies/local storage for authentication, CSRF protection, and UI preferences (such as theme). These are used to operate your self-hosted instance.

Third-party services

Thoth does not require third-party analytics by default. If you configure external services (reverse proxy, email, integrations, etc.), their privacy policies apply.

Contact

For privacy questions or issues, open a report on GitHub Issues.
